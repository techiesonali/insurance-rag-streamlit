Motor Insurance Policy – Accidental Damage

Coverage:
The policy covers accidental physical damage to the insured vehicle arising from
collision, overturning, fire, natural events, and other covered accidental events.

Claim Intimation:
The policyholder should notify the insurer as soon as reasonably possible after
an incident. The claimant should provide the policy number, vehicle registration
number, incident date, incident location, and a description of the incident.

Exclusions:
Damage caused intentionally by the policyholder is not covered. Normal wear and
tear, mechanical breakdown without an insured event, and use of the vehicle for
unauthorized purposes are excluded.

Required Documents:
The claimant may be required to provide the claim form, policy document, vehicle
registration certificate, driving licence, photographs of the damaged vehicle,
and supporting incident documentation.
