Claims Processing Guidelines

First Notice of Loss:
The claims team should verify the policy number and establish whether the policy
was active on the date of loss.

Initial Assessment:
The claims officer should collect incident details, identify the type of damage,
check applicable coverage, and determine whether additional investigation is
required.

Investigation:
Potential fraud indicators, inconsistent incident descriptions, suspicious
documentation, or unusual claim patterns should be escalated for investigation.

Settlement:
A claim should only proceed to settlement after coverage validation, document
verification, assessment of the loss, and required approvals.

Escalation:
Claims requiring investigation or exceeding the applicable authority threshold
should be escalated to the designated claims authority.
